## Problem statement — Version 1
College students searching for internships often have to look across multiple platforms, college groups, and personal networks. This can make it difficult to identify opportunities that match their skills and preferences. We want to explore whether a focused discovery and comparison experience could reduce that effort.

## Intended build
We intend to build a lightweight internship discovery prototype that helps students filter opportunities and compare a small number of relevant options side by side. This is an initial direction, not a commitment to a full platform or an AI recommendation engine.
