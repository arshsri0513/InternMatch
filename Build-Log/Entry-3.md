# Build Log — Entry 3: Brief and Verify

## Six-part research brief

**Context:** We are exploring whether college students experience enough friction when searching for internships to justify a focused discovery/comparison product.

**Objective:** Determine what students actually do today, what parts of the search are difficult, and whether fragmentation or comparison is a meaningful problem.

**Task:** Research existing evidence and organise it into user behaviour, current alternatives, pain points, and gaps. Identify claims that require field validation.

**Constraints:** Do not invent statistics or quotes. Separate sourced facts from inference, hypothesis, and assumption. Prefer recent and directly relevant evidence. Do not treat AI-generated personas as evidence.

**Output:** A structured research summary with claims, sources, open questions, and a list of claims requiring field validation.

**Success criteria:** Important factual claims are traceable to an original source; unsupported claims are clearly marked; the research identifies what remains unknown.

## Questions AI should ask before research
The following questions were intentionally recorded as open questions rather than answered by invention:
1. Which student year/discipline is the primary ICP?
2. Which geography should the research represent?
3. Which internship types are in scope?
4. What does "relevant" mean for the target user?
5. What evidence threshold will be sufficient to justify the prototype?
